Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Overview

My Perfect Party (also known as Moja savršena proslava) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android app.

Local data

The app does not require an account. Your event data — including guests, seating, expenses, tasks, and notes — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal planning data, and the developer does not have access to your content.

Optional backup

If you use the email backup feature, a JSON backup file is sent using your device mail tools to the address you choose. That email is handled by your email provider.

Ads

The free version may show ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage data according to their own policies. Learn more at Google’s advertising policies.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission for reminders, and permission to save invitation images you create to your gallery. Photos are not uploaded to our servers.

Children

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13.
